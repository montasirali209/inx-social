# OpenMontage integration notice

INXSocial's Stock Video Creator follows the documentary-montage production workflow published by OpenMontage: plan a script and scenes, retrieve licensed stock footage, compose a finished edit, validate it, and retain source provenance.

https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage

The INXSocial service uses an isolated integration boundary and identifies OpenMontage-derived workflow metadata in each stock-video production. Pexels and Pixabay media remain subject to their respective content licences and contributor rights; every selected clip is recorded in the production provenance.
