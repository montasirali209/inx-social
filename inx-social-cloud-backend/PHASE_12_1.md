# Phase 12.1 — Multi-Page Posts and Bulk Scheduler

This phase separates destination choice from account connection and turns Posts into a direct publishing workspace.

- Create text, image or video posts without entering AI Content Studio.
- Choose one or several connected Facebook Pages for each post.
- Publish immediately or choose an exact local date and time.
- Create one independently tracked publishing job per Page.
- Upload one selected image or video safely to every chosen destination.
- View accurate title, media type, destination and status in Posts.
- Rename Scheduler to Bulk Scheduler and choose multiple Pages per session.
- Check duplicates, occupied Meta slots and schedule capacity separately per Page.
- Remove the universal Active Page control from the customer interface.
- Keep destination selection local to Posts, Bulk Scheduler, Analytics and AI Content Studio.

No database migration, Railway variable, Mac gateway change or new dependency is required.
