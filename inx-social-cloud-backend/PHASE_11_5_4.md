# Phase 11.5.4 — Simple Post Review and First-Post Intelligence

This incremental release requires Phase 11.5.3.

## Customer experience

- Replaces the dense review popup with a focused image, caption, date/time and approval flow.
- Places one plain-language image instruction field directly below the preview.
- Removes image-prompt shortcut chips.
- Moves title, accessibility text and creative direction into collapsed Advanced details.
- Separates publishing date and time and saves edits before approval.
- Blocks expired publishing times before approval.

## First-post behavior

- Recognises first, welcome, introduction and launch-post missions.
- Defaults first-post imagery to Quality generation.
- Requires a benefit-led product or business introduction rather than a dashboard feature dump.
- Requests a concise opening caption, supported customer benefits, a gentle call to action and no more than four hashtags.
- Requires a product-relevant launch visual without generated logos, interface mockups or unsupported claims.

## Media diagnostics

- Carries rejected visual-review issues into Campaign Review.
- Distinguishes an unavailable private image model from a generic worker pause.
- Preserves the hard rule that rejected images cannot be approved.

No database migration or Mac service change is included.
