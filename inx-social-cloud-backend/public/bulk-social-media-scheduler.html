Bulk social media scheduling questions

What is bulk social media scheduling?

It means preparing and scheduling multiple posts as a batch rather than creating every scheduled item from scratch one by one.

Can I schedule photos and videos in bulk?

INXSocial supports batch publishing workflows for social photos and videos, while retaining the destinations and scheduling details for each post.

Can different posts go to different accounts?

Yes. Destination selection is handled per post, allowing a batch to cover different connected accounts where the relevant integration is supported.

Where do I review the batch after scheduling?

Scheduled content remains part of the wider INXSocial publishing and calendar workflow so you can review queue activity and publishing status.
