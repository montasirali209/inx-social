Bulk social media scheduling questions

Which social networks does INXSocial support?

Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky and X are available through the current connected-account publishing workflow. Formats vary by network.

Can I schedule photos and videos in bulk?

INXSocial supports batch publishing workflows for social photos and videos where the selected network supports that content type.

Can different posts go to different accounts?

Yes. Destination selection is handled per post, allowing one batch to cover different connected accounts and networks.

Where do I review the batch after scheduling?

Scheduled content remains part of the wider INXSocial publishing and calendar workflow so you can review queue activity and publishing status.
