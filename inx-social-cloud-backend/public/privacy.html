Privacy Policy

How INAXX LTD collects, uses, stores and protects information in connection with INX Social.

Last updated: 13 July 2026

This policy describes the INX Social production service, including customer accounts, billing and customer-authorised Meta Page publishing.

1. Who we are

INX Social is provided by INAXX LTD, United Kingdom. References to “we”, “us” or “our” mean INAXX LTD.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email address and authentication information.
  • Subscription, licence and device activation information.
  • Facebook Page identifiers, Page names, access credentials or tokens received through an authorised Meta connection.
  • Publishing instructions, captions, schedule times and status information.
  • Technical logs, IP address, device information and diagnostic events.
  • Messages sent to support or through our contact channels.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and secure the INX Social service.
  • Authenticate users and activate authorised devices.
  • Identify Pages a user is permitted to manage.
  • Check publishing status and scheduled Page activity.
  • Create or manage posts requested by the user.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate failures and provide support.
  • Manage trials, subscriptions and service communications.

4. Meta Platform Data

Meta Platform Data is used only to provide customer-authorised Page connection, Page selection, publishing status, scheduled-slot checking and customer-requested publishing functions. We do not sell Meta Platform Data or use it for unrelated advertising.

5. Legal bases

Depending on the context, we process information to perform a contract, pursue legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, comply with legal obligations, or act with consent.

6. Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers supporting hosting, authentication, email, payments, diagnostics and customer support. We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

7. Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed for the service, legal compliance, dispute resolution and security. Account and Platform Data deletion can be requested using our published deletion instructions.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational controls, including restricted access, secure transport and credential protection. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal information.

10. Contact

Email privacy enquiries to contact@inaxx.co.uk.
