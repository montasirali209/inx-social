Social media content calendar questions

What is a social media content calendar?

It is a visual view of planned and scheduled posts that helps you understand what is publishing, when it is due and how your wider schedule is balanced.

Can the calendar work with multiple connected accounts?

Yes. The calendar can combine scheduled INXSocial publications with available published feed history from connected accounts, including posts published natively outside INXSocial.

Does the calendar show publishing status?

Yes. The calendar can show published, scheduled, needs-review and failed activity. Historical feed posts are read-only, while supported scheduled INXSocial posts can retain reschedule or remove actions.

How does bulk scheduling fit with the calendar?

Bulk scheduling prepares multiple posts efficiently; the content calendar then helps you review how those scheduled posts fit into the wider publishing plan.
