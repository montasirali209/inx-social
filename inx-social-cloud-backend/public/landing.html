No. Scheduling is one part of INXSocial. It is an all-in-one social media management workspace for connected accounts, content creation, single and bulk scheduling, calendars, media, publishing status, analytics and AI-assisted creation.

You can connect Facebook Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts through Meta, or connect an Instagram professional profile directly. LinkedIn identity connection is also available, with additional publishing connectors continuing to roll out.

Create general posts, photo posts, videos and Reels where the connected platform permissions support them. Bulk Scheduler lets you prepare multiple media items and captions in one workflow.

AI Content Studio is included across INXSocial plans and provides Image Post, Carousel Post, Short Video / Reel and UGC Ad Post workflows. Short Video / Reel can open either AI Generated Video or Stock Video Creator; each workflow uses the shared AI credit wallet.

Video Clipping is an upcoming INXSocial feature and is not being presented as a live capability yet. The planned workflow is intended to turn longer videos into reusable short-form social clips.

Creator, Pro, Business and Agency all include the complete publishing, analytics, caption and AI Content Studio workflow. Higher plans increase connected-account capacity and monthly AI credits, with Priority support from Pro and Priority+ on Agency.

Stripe securely handles checkout, invoices and payment-method updates. INXSocial does not display or store your full payment-card details.

Landing-page plan buttons take you directly to Billing & Plans inside your workspace, where you can review or change your subscription through the Stripe-powered flow.
