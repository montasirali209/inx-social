# Phase 12.2 — Professional Posts Workspace

Phase 12.2 rebuilds Posts as a permanent professional publishing workspace.

- Clicking **Posts** opens the complete composer immediately; no second click is required.
- The composer supports text, image and video/Reel posts, drag-and-drop media, multiple Page destinations, publishing now and scheduling for later.
- Status cards, filters and the publication table are driven by real saved data without invented growth percentages.
- The layout includes an honest Facebook preview and clearly marks planned connectors instead of pretending they are live.
- AI caption assistance opens AI Content Studio, while content scoring and recommended scheduling remain inside Posts.
- The layout is responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile sizes.

No database migration, Mac gateway change or new environment variable is required.
